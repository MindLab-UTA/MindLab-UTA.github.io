Biography

Dr. Crystal Cooper is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Communication. Her research focuses on digital media, interpersonal communication, and the impact of emerging technologies on modern society. She has published numerous peer-reviewed articles and is dedicated to fostering an interactive and inclusive classroom environment.

Education

  • M.A. in Media Studies – University of Michigan
  • B.A. in Journalism – University of Texas at Arlington

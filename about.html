Our mentor

Dr. Crystal M. Cooper

  • Assistant Professor of Psychology, UT Arlington
  • Principal Investigator, Neuroscience Research Center
  • Principal Investigator, Jane and John Justin Neurosciences Center, Cook Children's
  • Adjunct Assistant Professor of Psychiatry, UT Southwestern Medical Center
UTA faculty profile Publications (NCBI) ResearchGate LinkedIn

Dr. Cooper is a cognitive neuroscientist originally from San Diego, California. She completed all of her degrees at UT Arlington, trained and worked in psychiatry at UT Southwestern, and joined Cook Children's as a principal investigator in 2020. Her work uses brain imaging and behavioral testing to find measurable signs of disease risk, disease state, and treatment response, with a focus on children and teens.

Research

  • Adding MRI and behavioral phenotyping to studies of neurological conditions in children, such as epilepsy and cerebral palsy.
  • Extending that work to pediatric mood and anxiety disorders.
  • Emotion processing and cognitive control in adolescents with epilepsy, including links to depression.
  • Earlier work: tasks that measure memory deficits across the lifespan, and MRI studies of brain and behavior in veterans with Gulf War Illness.

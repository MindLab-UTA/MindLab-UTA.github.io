Welcome to MindLab @ UTA

MindLab is a student-led group at the University of Texas at Arlington, mentored by Dr. Crystal M. Cooper. We bring together coursework, research ideas, and community-minded projects in psychology and neuroscience.

Mental health

Understanding mood and anxiety in young people.
